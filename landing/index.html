How it works

1

Publish or search

Hosts publish a room, seekers search by neighborhood, price, and availability.

2

Apply

Seekers apply with a short message, hosts review and shortlist who they like.

3

Visit and move in

Shortlisted seekers visit the room, and the host accepts one to move in.
